# Literature Survey: Decidability Landscape and Non-Termination Technology for Valuation-Branched Collatz-Like Machines

Agent-produced survey, July 25, 2026.

## Part A: Decidability landscape

### A1. Kurtz–Simon and the boundary of decidable Collatz-like classes

Kurtz & Simon (TAMC 2007; [full paper](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~simon/RES/collatz.pdf)) define a *Collatz function* g with modulus p, rational a_i, b_i: g(x) = a_i·x + b_i when x ≡ i (mod p), integrality required.

- **Theorem 1.4:** given a Collatz function g, it is undecidable whether for all x there is an i with g^(i)(x) = 1.
- **Theorem 1.5:** {g : range-g = ω} is **Pi-0-2-complete** (totality of "reaches 1" over all starts).
- Builds on **Conway 1972** ("Unpredictable iterations"): undecidable whether all starts 2^k reach 1; Conway's construction uses *purely multiplicative* maps g(n) = a_i·n on residue classes (FRACTRAN's ancestor); single-orbit halting over this class is **Sigma-0-1-complete**, so single-orbit non-halting (our conjecture type) is **Pi-0-1-complete as a class**.

**Decidable islands:** no branching (deterministic affine loop, any dimension): termination decidable over R (Tiwari CAV 2004), Q (Braverman CAV 2006), Z (Hosseini–Ouaknine–Worrell ICALP 2019). One counter: reachability decidable (Haase–Kreutzer–Ouaknine–Worrell 2009). Contracting/injective 1D maps: see A3. Strict contraction: orbits eventually periodic. Our maps are expanding valuation-branched — on the far shore from every island. Missing in the literature and available to us: a *small universal machine in our exact format* (A2), turning "in a hard class" into "our syntax at size k is universal; our cryptids live below k."

### A2. Conway/FRACTRAN universality and minimal sizes

- **Conway 1987 (FRACTRAN)** universal; PRIMEGAME = 14 fractions. Conway 1972 gives universality for offset-free rational-multiplier maps with halting = "orbit hits a power of 2" — *structurally identical to our halting criteria*. Note: Conway's universal maps also have 2-recognizable target sets, so sparse-recognizable halting sets do **not** push into a decidable fragment.
- **Ben-Amram (STACS 2013 / Computability 2015), Theorem 9** ([PDF](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol020-stacs2013/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.514/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.514.pdf)): "There is a constant m such that GCP restricted to modulus m is Pi-0-2-complete" — **fixed-modulus universal Collatz maps exist** (m from a universal register machine). Sharpest known "small universal Collatz"; optimal m never computed.
- **Small-device frontier** (Woods–Neary survey [arXiv:1110.2230](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.2230); Margenstern): below known universality sizes sit machines that iterate Collatz — size lower bounds there are Collatz-hard. **Stérin–Woods** ([arXiv:2107.12475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.12475)): explicit 15-state 2-symbol TM halts iff Erdős's conjecture ("2^n has a base-3 digit 2 for all n > 8") is false.
- **Two-counter caveat**: Minsky 2CMs universal only under exponential input encoding; **Schroeppel 1972** (MIT AI Memo 257), Barzdin: a 2CM cannot compute 2^N from N ("weak" universality in Korec's classification; certified analysis Dudenhefner FSCD 2022). With 3+ counters the caveat vanishes; our 4-counter machines face no structural obstruction to ambient-class universality.
- **Koiran–Moore** (TCS 1999): analytic closed-form maps universal already in dimension 1.

**Assessment.** Achievable project: smallest *universal* machine in our guarded-counter syntax (Ben-Amram Thm 9 + small universal register machine), giving the frontier "universal at (counters, rules) = (c,r); our cryptids at (4,11) and below" — the formal-hardness deliverable in the BB(15) style.

### A3. Piecewise affine reachability: the MSB/LSB fault line

- **Koiran–Cosnard–Garzon 1994:** PAM reachability undecidable in dimension 2; dimension 1 open — still open, even for **two intervals** (Kurganskyy–Potapov, SOFSEM 2016 / [arXiv:1510.04121](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04121)).
- **Decidable 1D subclasses:** *injective* 1D PAMs — reachability **unconditionally decidable** (Ghahremani–Kelmendi–Ouaknine, LICS 2023, [arXiv:2301.09752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.09752); connects the general case to Diophantine properties of transcendental numbers, analogous to Positivity for LRS). Kurganskyy–Potapov: p-adic-weight decidability for further classes; PAM orbits tied to rational-base representations. Bellman-operator PAMs: dim 2 decidable (Varonka et al., MFCS 2025, [arXiv:2502.19923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19923)).
- **Ben-Amram (STACS 2013):** over Z with pieces = convex rational polyhedra (interval pieces in 1D, no congruence conditions — his Definition 3): mortality **PSPACE-complete in dim 1**, **Pi-0-2-complete in dim 2** — the 2D undecidability proof works *by simulating a generalized Collatz map*, using the second dimension for division/remainder.

**Synthesis (the fault line).** The open/decidable 1D results all concern *interval-branched* maps (branch chosen by magnitude — most-significant-digit information). Our maps are *valuation-branched* (least-significant-digit information) — exactly the mechanism Conway used for universality and Ben-Amram needed a second dimension to simulate with intervals. Boundary theorem: **1D interval-branched PAM reachability is open (decidable under injectivity); 1D valuation-branched affine reachability is Sigma-0-1-complete as a class, even at fixed modulus.** Our maps sit strictly on the undecidable side; the 1D PAM techniques (transcendence, β-expansions) do not transfer — their expansion reveals leading digits, our dynamics consume trailing digits. No generic decidability result is possible; only instance-level work or new certificate classes (B7).

### A4. Skolem-type problems and our exponential-polynomial halting equations

Skolem decidable for algebraic LRS order ≤ 3 and real-algebraic order 4 (Mignotte–Shorey–Tijdeman 1984, Vereshchagin 1985); order-4 completed for all algebraic LRS ([Bacik, TheoretiCS 2025](https://theoretics.episciences.org/17020/pdf)). "Skolem meets Schanuel" (MFCS 2022): simple LRS decidable assuming Skolem conjecture + p-adic Schanuel, with a certifying tool. **Universal Skolem sets** (Luca–Ouaknine–Worrell, LICS 2021 on; ["Skolem meets Bateman-Horn" arXiv:2308.01152](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.01152)): positive-density universal set unconditionally; density 1 under Bateman–Horn. [Ouaknine, "The Skolem Landscape," ICALP 2023 invited](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2023.5).

**Connection to our halting families.** Halting conditions are affine in (D, 2^n, n). (1) For any *fixed branch pattern* the halting condition is u_n = A·2^n + P(n) = 0 or Pillai-type c·a^n − 2^k = d: LRS-with-dominant-root zero problems and 2/3-term S-unit equations — **decidable, effectively, via Baker**. Umbrella: existential fragment of (Z; 0, 1, <, +, 2^N, 3^N) decidable (Karimov–Luca–Nieuwveld–Ouaknine–Worrell, [arXiv:2407.05191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05191)); complexity of Presburger + powers ([Benedikt–Chistikov–Mansutti, ICALP 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03037)). Retro-explains why per-family sparse halting characterizations succeeded: each accelerated segment lives in a decidable exponential-Diophantine theory. (2) **The undecidability lives entirely in the unbounded alternation of branches** — the iteration — not in the arithmetic of any halting equation. Skolem-hard reductions into our families are the wrong direction (dominant-root Skolem is the easy case); the correct analogy is **Positivity-style conditional hardness** — theorem template: "a decision procedure for family X would resolve open instances of [Matthews' Uniform Distribution conjecture / Carelli's Reachability Conjecture]" (see B6).

## Part B: Termination/non-termination technology

### B5. Non-termination provers: certificate shapes, and a proof they cannot work for us

**What exists.** Recurrent sets via lasso + Farkas constraints ([Gupta–Henzinger–Majumdar–Rybalchenko–Xu, POPL 2008](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1328438.1328459)); closed recurrence sets (Chen–Cook–Fuhs–Nimkar–O'Hearn, TACAS 2014; in T2); **geometric nontermination arguments** ([Leike–Heizmann, arXiv:1609.05207](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05207)): infinite run written explicitly as sums of geometric series, complete for deterministic conjunctive linear loops with nonnegative eigenvalues; [LoAT (Frohn–Giesl, FMCAD 2019)](http://theory.stanford.edu/~barrett/fmcad/papers/FMCAD2019_paper_39.pdf) non-termination via loop acceleration; RevTerm (PLDI 2021) polynomial Positivstellensatz certificates; Ultimate, AProVE similar.

**Confirmed impossibility for us.** Certificate languages are exactly: (a) Presburger/polyhedral sets ± linear congruences, (b) semialgebraic sets, (c) explicit eventually-geometric orbits. In our one-variable return coordinate over N: (a) Presburger subsets of N are eventually periodic — congruence invariants up to finite corrections, and expansion kills finite corrections, so **our no-congruence theorems formally rule out every linear-arithmetic recurrent set**; (b) semialgebraic subsets of Z in 1D are finite unions of intervals — every infinite interval meets our unbounded halting-adjacent sets; (c) a geometric certificate forces an exact LRS orbit, contradicting mantissa equidistribution (checkable per machine). The tools' other mode (divergence witnesses: "x grows, guard eventually false") is inapplicable — our guards are equalities against sparse sets: we need *avoidance*, not growth. **No existing tool's certificate class can express "orbit avoids a 2-recognizable sparse set"; the missing class is automatic/regular sets (B7).**

### B6. Termination analysis of conjectured-nonterminating number-theoretic loops

- Frohn–Giesl (CAV 2019, [arXiv:1905.08664](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.08664)): triangular affine integer loops decidable. Frohn–Hark–Giesl ([arXiv:1910.11588](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11588)): twn polynomial loops decidable over R; over Z/Q only non-termination semi-decidability. Hosseini–Ouaknine–Worrell: full affine over Z. *Branch-free deterministic loops are a solved decidable world*; 2-path loops over Z already undecidable (Ben-Amram–Genaim–Masud, TOPLAS 2012).
- **Carelli, "Loop Termination and Generalized Collatz Sequences" (ICALP 2026, [arXiv:2605.15094](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15094)) — keystone recent result:** for one-variable single-path linear-constraint loops (convex nondeterministic transition relation) over Z: (i) any cycle implies a cycle of length ≤ 2; (ii) termination decidable in PTIME *assuming his Reachability Conjecture* — a weakening of **Matthews' Uniform Distribution conjecture** ("every unbounded generalized Collatz sequence is uniformly distributed mod d^α"), stating every weak-Collatz sequence reaches a certain residue class mod d; (iii) conversely, any decision procedure for one-variable loops would settle open instances. Proved for d = 2; all d > 2 open. Machinery: Minkowski–Weyl analysis of the transition polyhedron; the hard corner case is a trace forming *a weak Collatz sequence avoiding a residue class* — the exact dual of our "orbit avoids a recognizable set." CISPA group (thanks Ouaknine in acknowledgments) actively working on it. Two concrete interactions: check whether our return-map families instantiate his weak Collatz mappings (our proven characterizations would give new solved instances beyond d = 2); check whether his d = 2 technique transfers to any of our machines.
- Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule ([arXiv:2105.14697](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.14697)): state of the art for automated termination of Collatz proper; matrix search saturates at ~12×12.
- **Certified non-trivial non-termination:** the genuine success story is [Coq-BB5 / BB(5) (arXiv:2509.12337)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.12337): ~181M machines with machine-checked certificates, including **FAR** (regular language closed under step, containing initial, avoiding halting; 23 machines), plus [Xu's Skelet #17](https://chrisxudoesmath.com/papers/skelet17.pdf) — deepest certified non-termination of a number-theory-flavored machine to date. No verification-community tool has certified non-halting of an open-problem-grade number-theoretic loop.

### B7. Büchi arithmetic, valuation predicates, and where decidability exactly fails

- **Büchi–Bruyère:** (N, +, V_q) decidable; definable = q-recognizable (survey: Bruyère–Hansel–Michaux–Villemaire 1994). **Semenov:** (N, +, x -> 2^x) decidable. **Villemaire:** (N, +, V_2, V_3) undecidable; **Cobham–Semenov:** recognizable in two multiplicatively independent bases => semilinear. Presburger + power-of-2 in ~3EXP ([ICALP 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03037)). Existential (Z; +, <, 2^N, 3^N) decidable ([arXiv:2407.05191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05191)). MSO: Elgot–Rabin 1966; **Berthé–Karimov–Nieuwveld–Ouaknine–Vahanwala–Worrell, LICS 2024 Distinguished Paper ([arXiv:2405.07953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07953)): MSO(N; <, Pow2, Pow3) decidable**; Nieuwveld–Ouaknine (MFCS 2025, [arXiv:2507.16581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16581)): extension to LRS value-set predicates.
- **Where iteration kills it:** **Dhiman–Pandey ([arXiv:2602.06066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06066), Feb 2026):** for the family T_{q,d} (q, d odd, q + d a power of 2 — includes 3x+1), the *reachability relation* is **not FO-definable in Büchi arithmetic (N, +, V_q)**; proof via Cobham (definable reachability would define powers of 2 in base q), notably without embedding computation. Plus Blumensath–Grädel 2000: FO decidable on automatic structures, FO + transitive closure not. Diagnosis: **each single step of F and our halting sets are Büchi-definable (v2 guards + affine updates are exactly (N,+,V2) material); the transitive closure is what leaves the decidable logic.**

**The actionable gap.** Non-definability of the reachability *relation* does not forbid a definable *invariant*. A 2-automatic set I with (i) s0 in I, (ii) F(I) ⊆ I, (iii) I ∩ Halt = ∅ is a finite non-halting certificate — and each condition is a decidable statement of Büchi arithmetic (F is a base-2 rational transduction; regular image, inclusion, emptiness all effective; **Walnut can literally check candidates**). This is FAR ported from TM-configuration space into Büchi arithmetic — and it is **not ruled out by our no-congruence theorems**: eventually periodic (congruence) sets are the degenerate special case of automatic sets; general automatic invariants can encode digit-pattern conservation laws far beyond congruences — exactly the q-adic memory structure the Hydra analysis found. Regular model checking (Bouajjani–Jonsson–Nilsson–Touili, CAV 2000; Abdulla's surveys) provides the search toolbox.

## The three most promising leads (agent's ranking)

**Lead 1 — Automatic-invariant certificates (new proof tool; implementable immediately).** Search for 2-automatic (LSB-first) invariant sets for each machine's return map: SAT/enumeration over small DFAs, certificate conditions checked by automata products (Walnut-compatible). A success is a complete machine-checkable non-halting proof — the first certificate class not excluded by the no-congruence theorems. Failures are theorems too: "no 2-automatic invariant with ≤ N states" is decidable per machine — generalizing the no-congruence results, the FAR-fails-on-cryptids phenomenon now in our setting. Dhiman–Pandey constrains the relation, not the invariant: no known obstruction.

**Lead 2 — Decidability-boundary/size-frontier theorem for our exact class.** Conway offset-free universality + Kurtz–Simon Pi-0-2-completeness + Ben-Amram fixed-modulus Thm 9 => construct a small universal machine in our guarded-counter syntax; state the boundary theorem (interval-branched 1D open/decidable-fragments vs valuation-branched 1D Sigma-0-1-complete even at fixed modulus), with our cryptids located strictly below the universal size. The citable "where our machines sit" result, BB(15) genre; slots into the meta-report's ranking.

**Lead 3 — The Carelli/Matthews bridge and Baker-effective partial non-halting proofs.** Determine whether our families furnish instances of Carelli's weak Collatz mappings (either direction), and whether his d = 2 method transfers. In parallel, import Baker-effective linear-forms bounds (the engine behind arXiv:2407.05191 and LICS 2024 MSO) into the log2(5/4) mantissa backbone: for machines whose halting families are 2/3-term S-unit conditions, effective bounds may convert sparse-halting characterizations into finite verified computations — full unconditional non-halting proofs for the most structured family members.

**Explicit negative findings (to save future effort):** 1D PAM decidability literature does not transfer (wrong side of the MSB/LSB fault line); Skolem-hardness reductions into our families implausible (our equations are dominant-root = the decidable case; pursue Positivity-style conditional hardness instead); all current non-termination tools provably inexpressive (B5).
